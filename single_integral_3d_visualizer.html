1 Partitioning $[a, b]$ into Width $\Delta x$

We divide the closed interval $[a, b]$ on the $x$-axis into $n$ equal subintervals $[x_{i-1}, x_i]$, each of uniform width:

$$\Delta x = \frac{b - a}{n} \;\longrightarrow\; dx$$
2 Area Element $dA = f(x)\,dx$

At a sample point $x_i^* \in [x_{i-1}, x_i]$, we erect a thin rectangular strip (shown in gold) of height $f(x_i^*)$ and width $\Delta x$:

$$\Delta A_i = f(x_i^*)\,\Delta x \;\longrightarrow\; dA = f(x)\,dx$$
3 Signed Area ($+$ Above, $-$ Below)

Where $f(x) > 0$, strips rise above the $x$-axis (cyan, positive area). Where $f(x) < 0$, strips extend below the $x$-axis (rose, negative signed area).

4 Limit as $n \to \infty$ ($\Delta x \to 0$)

Summing all $n$ strips and taking the limit as $n \to \infty$ eliminates the stair-step error, yielding the exact definite integral:

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^{n} f(x_i^*)\,\Delta x$$
